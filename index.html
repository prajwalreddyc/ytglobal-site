Who we are

A consulting firm that pairs technical depth with business context.

YT Global Inc. is an IT consulting firm based in Wilmington, Delaware. Our consultants have worked in healthcare, public health, insurance and technology, where data has to be accurate, traceable and secure.

We help clients close the gap between what their systems could do and what they do today. That means clear designs, working increments and documentation your own staff can use after we leave.

Services

Consulting practices for every layer of your technology.

Most engagements combine two or three practices.

Cloud and infrastructure

  • Cloud services

    We plan and carry out the move of applications and data to the cloud, then help you scale, secure and monitor them. You receive a target design, a migration plan and cost controls before anything moves.

    Migration · Architecture · Cost control

  • IT support and managed services

    Onsite and remote support for backup, recovery, upgrades and troubleshooting, with runbooks your own staff can follow.

    Monitoring · Recovery · Upgrades

Data and analytics

  • Data engineering

    Pipelines that load, validate and reconcile data on a schedule. Each one is monitored, logged and safe to rerun after a failure.

    Integration · Quality · Automation

  • Data warehousing and analytics

    Warehouse and lakehouse designs that give analysts one agreed version of the data, built to fit your volumes and budget.

    Modeling · Warehousing · Big data

  • Business intelligence and reporting

    Dashboards and reports that finance, operations and program teams open every day, with metric definitions written down so numbers match.

    Dashboards · Metrics · Self-service

Applications and automation

  • Custom application development

    Web and mobile applications, from small internal tools to large enterprise systems, built so your own team can maintain them.

    Web · Mobile · Enterprise

  • Integration and automation

    Connections between your systems and automated deployment, so data moves on its own and a release becomes a routine event.

    Interfaces · Scheduling · Deployment

Approach

Four phases, each with something you can review.

  1. Phase 1

    Assess

    We inventory sources, volumes, owners and current pain points, and agree with you on what success looks like.

  2. Phase 2

    Design

    We draft the target architecture, data model, security approach and cost estimate for your review.

  3. Phase 3

    Build

    We deliver in working increments, tested in a separate environment before anything reaches production.

  4. Phase 4

    Run

    We hand over documentation and runbooks, or stay on to operate the platform alongside your team.
